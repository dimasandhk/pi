# Standards and proper scope

## Main reporting backbone: SEGRESS
Kitchenham, Madeyski & Budgen, *IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering* 49(3), 2023, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TSE.2022.3174092
Summary: https://ebse.webspace.durham.ac.uk/reporting/
- Domain-specific for software engineering secondary studies, including qualitative/mixed studies, systematic mapping, and SLR.
- Organizes title/abstract/introduction/methods/results/discussion and transparency/ethical reporting.
- Distinguish **risk of bias in primary studies** from certainty/confidence in a synthesized finding. Reporting of methodological quality alone is not identical to an established RoB appraisal.
- In the manuscript, name a study-specific 'quality appraisal' honestly if that is what the review actually performed.

## Complement: PRISMA 2020
https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020
https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-explanation-elaboration
https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-flow-diagram
- Reporting checklist of 27 items; report selection-flow stages.
- Distinguish **record**, **report**, **study**. Multiple papers may report one study; one paper may report more than one study.
- Show search records, pre-screen removals, screened records, full-text reports sought and retrieved, reasons for exclusion, studies/reports included.
- If automation removed records, state how, where, and any human audit; never imply reviewers independently screened automated results without evidence.
- 'PRISMA-compliant' only after an item-by-item applicability check.

## Detail: PRISMA-S
Rethlefsen et al., 2021: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z
- Complementary 16-item guidance for **reporting searches**.
- Require platform/database, search dates, exact per-platform strings and syntax, all limitations and filters, source differentiation, dedup tools/process, reference chasing, and full strategies in supplemental material.
- Report actual source failure/access limits (e.g., planned library not executed).
- Do not mistake PRISMA-S for a manual on how to develop a search.

## Conditional: SWiM
Campbell et al., 2020: https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.l6890
- 9-item reporting aid for synthesizing *quantitative intervention effects* without meta-analysis.
- Explain why no meta-analysis, how studies/outcomes are grouped, how findings are tabulated and interpreted, heterogeneity, robustness and synthesis limitations.
- No claim that a narrative summary by itself meets SWiM. It is not comprehensive reporting for qualitative thematic synthesis.

## Process framework
Kitchenham & Charters (2007); EBSE https://ebse.webspace.durham.ac.uk/planning/ and https://ebse.webspace.durham.ac.uk/reporting-dissemination/
- Planning, conducting, reporting, protocol change log.
- Capture what was performed vs what was specified.

## Narrative/thematic synthesis
Cruzes & Dybå (2011): https://doi.org/10.1109/ESEM.2011.36
- Code cross-paper findings, construct descriptive themes and more interpretive themes, review exceptions and compare evidence.
- Qualitative synthesis should involve reviewer scrutiny to reduce one-person interpretations.

## Abstract
https://ebse.webspace.durham.ac.uk/structured-abstracts/
- Use background/aims/method/results/conclusions when venue permits, concise and exact.

## Reporting quality research
Budgen et al. (2018): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2017.10.017
- Reporting problems particularly in assessment, synthesis, reviewer procedures, provenance. Audit these areas carefully.
